Privacy Policy

Size Match Personal Fit

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Developer: Alamgir App Studio (Alamgir Hossain), Bangladesh
App package: com.alamgirappstudio.sizematchpersonalfit

Size Match Personal Fit ("the app", "we", "us") helps you find the right clothing, shoe and accessory size using your own measurements and size charts. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and what choices you have. We designed the app so that your personal data stays on your phone.

In short: we do not have user accounts, we do not run a server that stores your data, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your personal information.

1. Information the app handles

Everything below is created by you inside the app and is stored only on your device:

  • Measurement profiles: names of profiles and body measurements you enter (for example height, chest, waist, hip, foot length), including profiles for family members or friends and kids' growth data.
  • Brand size profiles and favorite brands: the brands and sizes you save.
  • Saved size charts: charts you scan, paste, import from a link, or type in.
  • Size history and purchase fit history: what you bought, the size, and whether it fitted well.
  • Settings: fit preference, measurement unit, default profile and country choice.
  • Photos and screenshots: pictures you take or choose for the camera photo guide or the size chart scanner.
  • Backup files: ZIP files you or the automatic backup feature create.

We do not ask for, and the app does not collect, your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or any account login.

2. How each feature uses your information

Size converters, country converter, unit converter, regional size guide

Shoe, clothing, jeans/pants, ring, hat/cap, gloves, bra, socks, kids shoes and kids clothing converters work completely offline on your device. The country list comes from your phone. To pre-select a country, the app may read the country code of your SIM card, network or phone language settings. This is a general country code only (for example "BD" or "US"); it is not your precise location, it is used only on your device, and you can change it any time in Settings.

Measurement profiles, measurement guide and camera photo guide

Measurements are saved locally. The app does not request the CAMERA permission. When you choose to take a photo, the app opens your phone's own camera app, and the picture is returned to the app and saved in the app's private storage. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Size chart scanner (text recognition)

When you scan a size chart from a photo or screenshot, the text is read by Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your device (the recognition model is included in the app). The image is not sent to us or to Google for recognition.

Find My Size, product comparison, brand fit warning, confidence score

Matching of your measurements to a size chart is calculated on your device. No measurement is uploaded.

Product link import

If you paste or share a product link, the app connects directly from your phone to that web page (HTTPS only) to read its size table. The website you open will see a normal web request from your device, such as your IP address, as with any web browser. We do not receive this link or the page content. Use this feature only with websites you trust.

Share to app

When you share an image or text to the app from another app, the app only reads the item you shared, to open it in the scanner, link importer or chart editor.

Shopping size card, copy size, share size

These features create text or an image of your sizes. They are shared only when you press Copy or Share, and only with the app you choose in your phone's share menu. After sharing, that other app's own privacy policy applies.

Backup and restore

Backups are ZIP files containing the data you select. When you choose "Back up everything" or "Back up selected data", you decide where the file is saved using your phone's file picker. Automatic backups (daily, weekly or monthly) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone; the app keeps the most recent copies. We do not receive your backups. When you restore, the app first makes a safety copy of your current data. Keep backup files safe, because anyone who has the file can read what is in it.

3. Subscription and payments

Size Match Personal Fit is a subscription app. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your card number, payment details or Google account password. The app asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription so it can unlock the app, and may remember the result on your device for a short period so the app can work offline. Google processes your payment data under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service. You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

4. Permissions

5. Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing / Google Play services - subscription and payment.
  • Google ML Kit (text recognition, on-device) - reads text from size chart images on your phone.
  • AndroidX WorkManager - schedules automatic backups on your phone.

The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services that collect your personal data.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal information with anyone. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling. Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share it (for example by sharing your size card or saving a backup to cloud storage), or when the Google Play subscription check or a product link you request connects to its own server as described above.

7. Data storage, security and retention

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally access. The app only uses secure HTTPS connections and does not allow plain HTTP. We keep your data on your phone until you delete it. Because the app does not keep your data on any server, we cannot recover it for you if you lose your phone or delete the app without a backup. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app; use the app's own Backup feature instead.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data on your device. Please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any profile, brand, chart or history entry inside the app.
  • Delete all data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Size Match Personal Fit > Storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Delete backups from the "Backups on this phone" list, and delete any backup files you saved elsewhere.
  • Withdraw permissions at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • Cancel your subscription in Google Play at any time.

Since we do not collect or hold your personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to access, export or erase on your behalf. If you live in a region with data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA) and have a question about your rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's privacy

The app is a general shopping and sizing tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It includes kids' size converters and growth estimates, which are meant for parents and guardians to use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any child's measurements entered by a parent stay on the parent's phone.

10. Accuracy notice

Size recommendations, match percentages and confidence scores are close estimates based on the numbers you provide and general size tables. Brands differ, so always compare with the brand's own size chart. This notice is not related to privacy but is provided for your protection.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features. The new version will be posted at this page with an updated effective date. Please review it from time to time. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
